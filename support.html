FAQ

How do I cancel or change my ticket?
Contact the ferry operator directly (Balearia, FRS, AML, DFDS or Trasmediterranea) with your reference number.

I didn't receive my ticket
Check the spam folder. If not there, email us with the address used during purchase.

Do I need a Green Card to drive into Morocco?
Yes, mandatory. The app helps you arrange it via the official CAT Maroc portal.

Does the ticket work offline?
Yes. Once purchased, the ticket and QR work without internet.
